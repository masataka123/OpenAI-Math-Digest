# The Campana–Peternell conjecture in dimension six

**Catalogue 067: Fano sixfolds with nef tangent bundle**

This catalogue contains one manuscript. It treats complex dimension six of the general Campana–Peternell conjecture, combining known Picard-number and pseudoindex cases with characteristic-number constraints from two fibrations and VMRT recognition. It also derives a statement about ordinary universal covers of compact Kähler manifolds whose Albanese-fibre dimension is at most six.
