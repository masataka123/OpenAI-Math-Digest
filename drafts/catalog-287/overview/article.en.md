# All nonabelian free group factors are isomorphic

**Catalogue 287**

This single-paper catalogue follows the construction of small perturbations of free Haar generators and a generating limit, then the amplification step yielding $L(\mathbb F_2)\cong L(\mathbb F_3)$. It also covers the consequences for countably infinite rank, fundamental groups, and the dependence of free entropy dimensions on generators.
