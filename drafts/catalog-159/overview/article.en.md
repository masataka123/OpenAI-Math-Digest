# Erdős’s reciprocal-sum conjecture and quasipolynomial Szemerédi bounds

**From triangular density increments to progressions forced by divergent reciprocal sums.**

This catalogue concerns density bounds for sets avoiding arithmetic progressions of a fixed length, strong enough to force convergence of their reciprocal sums. The manuscript constructs density increments on triangular cells retaining earlier polynomial constraints, then controls dimension and width losses to close a finite iteration.
