# Yau’s uniformization conjecture

**Yau’s uniformization conjecture — Catalogue 338**

The official subject is Differential geometry. This single-manuscript catalogue concerns biholomorphic uniformization of complete noncompact Kähler manifolds with positive holomorphic bisectional curvature. The article follows the analytic and coordinate constructions used without additional geometric hypotheses.

## 2. Guide to the article

[Uniformization of complete Kähler manifolds with positive bisectional curvature](../yau-uniformization/article.en.md) connects flow construction, scalar comparison before completeness, the disc envelope, shrinking charts and surjectivity. The conclusion is biholomorphism of complex manifolds, not an isometric classification of the initial metric.
