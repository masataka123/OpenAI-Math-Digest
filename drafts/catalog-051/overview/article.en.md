## Entering the proof and its two consequences

Start with the [main proof overview](../canonical-ampleness/article.en.md#proof-overview), then follow [disc variations and the Hessian](../canonical-ampleness/article.en.md#proof-1), [nefness](../canonical-ampleness/article.en.md#proof-2), the [uniform volume bound](../canonical-ampleness/article.en.md#proof-3), and [positive intersection](../canonical-ampleness/article.en.md#proof-4). The overview also explains the subsequent steps through bigness and projectivity to ampleness.

After the main theorem, the [all-exponents generation result](../canonical-ampleness/article.en.md#proof-5) uses Fujita freeness from 038, while the [finite étale product cover](../canonical-ampleness/article.en.md#proof-6) uses universal-cover classification from 058. These inputs do not enter the proof of the main theorem.
