# Catalogue 340 — A counterexample to the nearby Lagrangian conjecture

**A counterexample to the nearby Lagrangian conjecture**

This single-manuscript catalogue belongs to the official subject Differential geometry. For some sufficiently large even $N$ and $Q=S^9\times S^{N-1}$, the manuscript claims a closed exact embedded Lagrangian diffeomorphic to the base but not Hamiltonian isotopic to the zero section. The article follows the nonzero tube class at infinity, the two-momentum embedding argument, and the contradiction obtained by transport to the zero section.
