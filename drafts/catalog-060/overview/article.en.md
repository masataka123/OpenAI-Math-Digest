# The Global Spherical Shell conjecture

**Catalogue 060**

This catalogue concerns the claim that every minimal class VII surface with positive second Betti number contains a global spherical shell. Its single manuscript constructs rational curves through weighted analysis on a cyclic cover and reduces the conclusion to established curve criteria.
