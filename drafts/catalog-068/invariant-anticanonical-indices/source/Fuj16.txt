KOLL ´AR–NADEL TYPE V ANISHING THEOREM
OSAMU FUJINO
Abstract. We prove an analytic generalization of Koll´ ar’s van-
ishing theorem, which contains the Nadel vanishing theorem as a
special case.
1. Introduction
In the conference, I talked about the Hodge theoretic aspect of in-
jectivity and vanishing theorems (see [
2], [ 3], and [ 4]). Here, I will
explain some analytic generalizations. In [ 5], Shin-ichi Matsumura and
I established the following theorems.

5, Theorem A]) . Let F be a holomorphic line bundle

Theorem 1.1 is a generalization of Enoki’s injectivity theorem (see
[1, Theorem 0.2]). Although the formulation of Theorem 1.1 may look
artiﬁcial, it has many interesting applications (see [ 5]). Theorem 1.2
below is a Bertini-type theorem for multiplier ideal sheaves.

The main purpose of this paper is to prove the following theorem,

Theorem 1.3 (Vanishing theorem of Koll´ ar–Nadel type). Let f : X →
Y be a holomorphic map from a compact K¨ ahler manifold X to a pro-
jective variety Y . Let F be a holomorphic line bundle on X equipped
with a singular hermitian metric h. Let H be an ample line bundle on
Y . Assume that there exists a smooth hermitian metric g on f ∗H such
that
√

for some ε > 0. Then we have
H i(Y, Rjf∗(ωX ⊗ F ⊗ J (h))) = 0
for every i > 0 and j, where ωX is the canonical bundle of X and J (h)
is the multiplier ideal sheaf associated to the singular her mitian metric
h.
We can easily see that Theorem 1.3 contains Demailly’s original for-
mulation of the Nadel vanishing theorem (see [ 5, Theorem 1.4]) and
Koll´ ar’s vanishing theorem (see [6, Theorem 2.1 (iii)]) as special cases.
Therefore, we call Theorem 1.3 the vanishing theorem of Koll´ ar–Nadel
type. For a related vanishing theorem, see [ 7, Theorem 1.3].
In this paper, we will freely use the same notation as in [ 5].

We close this section with a remark on Nakano semipositive vector

Remark. Let E be a Nakano semipositive vector bundle on X. We can
easily see that Theorem
1.3 holds even when ωX is replaced by ωX ⊗ E.
