# Tingley's sphere-isometry problem

**Catalogue 322**

This catalogue contains one manuscript claiming that a surjective isometry between unit spheres of real Banach spaces extends uniquely to a real-linear isometry of the spaces. The overview follows its proof by maximizing the distance defect between different radii, aligning chords, and eliminating the defect through convexity.

## Guide to the article

The overview of [A positive solution to Tingley’s problem](../tingley-positive-solution/article.en.md) first presents the overall proof of Theorem 1.1. It then explains the invariant closed convex set and decreasing noncompactness in Proposition 4.2, followed by the support and convexity contradiction in Proposition 5.3. Darbo’s fixed-point theorem and the Mazur–Ulam reflection argument are classical inputs; their application conditions are distinguished from the constructions supplied by the manuscript.
