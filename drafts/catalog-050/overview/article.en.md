# A counterexample to Griffiths’ positivity conjecture

**Catalogue 050**

The manuscript constructs a rank-two bundle on $\mathbf P^1\times\mathbf P^1$ whose family $E_m=f_m^*G\otimes\mathcal O(1,1)$ is ample for every positive integer $m$, while admitting no smooth strictly Griffiths-positive Hermitian metric for all sufficiently large $m$. This guide distinguishes the manuscript's claims from the scope of editorial checking.

## 2. Entry into the proof

The [individual article](../../papers/ample-quadric/) starts with the explicit construction of $G$ along three graphs. It then follows ampleness through two quotient sequences and a measure obstruction to a quadratic psh limit on the dual bundle. The graph identities connect the two arguments. The contradicted conjecture concerns a smooth strictly Griffiths-positive Hermitian metric on the ample bundle itself; neither exclusion of semipositive metrics nor an effective threshold is asserted.
