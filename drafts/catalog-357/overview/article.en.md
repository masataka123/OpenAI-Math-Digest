# Bi-Lipschitz coordinates at every regular RCD point

**Open coordinates at each prescribed regular point**

Official catalogue 357 concerns open bi-Lipschitz coordinates at every prescribed regular point of a noncollapsed $\operatorname{RCD}(K,n)$ space. It contains one manuscript. The proposed proof compensates for the scale-dependent degeneration of harmonic coordinates in their value space, obtaining all-pairs metric bounds and an open image.

The [article](../regular-rcd-coordinates/article.en.md) explains [how the two main inputs combine](../regular-rcd-coordinates/article.en.md#proof-overview), [summable directional pinching](../regular-rcd-coordinates/article.en.md#proof-1), [iteration in value space](../regular-rcd-coordinates/article.en.md#proof-2), and [all pairs, the limit and openness](../regular-rcd-coordinates/article.en.md#proof-3). The distinction from known a.e. rectifiability on measurable pieces is the ambient-open domain and same-dimensional open image at every prescribed regular point.

Theorem 1.1 in the manuscript states that $L_n$ depends **only on dimension**, while the neighborhood radius may depend on the space and point. This is distinguished from the official overview's abbreviated description of a point-dependent constant; the article and table follow the manuscript's quantifiers. Corollary 1.2 gives a full-measure open region containing all regular points and a countable atlas, without asserting that the exact regular locus is open.
