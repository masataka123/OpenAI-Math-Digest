 CATALOGUE 060




 The Global Spherical Shell conjecture
 This catalogue concerns the claim that every minimal class VII surface with positive second Betti
 number contains a global spherical shell. Its single manuscript constructs rational curves through
 weighted analysis on a cyclic cover and reduces the conclusion to established curve criteria.

 CATALOGUE 060 · PAPER 01 / 1 Overview




 Global Spherical Shells on Minimal
 Surfaces of Class VII

 Proving compactness of divisor components on a cyclic cover, then
 recovering a shell from the number of rational curves.

 The manuscript claims a proof of the Global Spherical Shell conjecture for minimal class VII
 surfaces with positive second Betti number. Its central step excludes noncompact components of
 effective divisors constructed from compactly supported Chern classes, using weighted Dolbeault
 cohomology and a cylinder estimate. This article follows that argument; it does not certify the
 correctness of the complete proof.
