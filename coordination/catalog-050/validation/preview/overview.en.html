For an explicit rank-two $G$, the bundle $E_m=f_m^*G\otimes\mathcal O_S(1,1)$ is ample for every $m\ge1$. There is an $m_0$ such that, for every $m\ge m_0$, it has no smooth strictly Griffiths-positive Hermitian metric.
