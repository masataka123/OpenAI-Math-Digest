Original paper (GitHub) ↗
Principal claim
From the empty infinite grid, Maker wins within 21 actual moves against every legal Breaker
continuation; the strategy also works on a 251-cell board and a 17×17 board.
Theorem 1 · p. 1; Corollary 6 · p. 8 ↗
Role and available overview
Compose ownership and exclusion conditions; a finite certificate yields an empty requirement
and height 21. Owning the common intersection preserves a continuation after every reply.
