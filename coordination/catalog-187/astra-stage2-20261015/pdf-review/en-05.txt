The 21-claim strategy is not a fixed sequence of local attacks. The manuscript recursively
combines sufficient conditions consisting of required Maker holdings and a region still free of
Breaker. If Maker owns the intersection of the child regions, every legal next reply misses at least
one child. A finite certificate iterates this principle to obtain a condition applicable to the empty
board; the choices in its proof are then made into one policy determined by the history.
